# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about the work, not the
person making it.

## Expected

- Disagree directly and specifically. "This benchmark doesn't measure what the README says
  it measures" is a good comment. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Accept correction without defensiveness, and give it without contempt.
- Respect that people contribute at different levels of experience and available time.
- Assume questions are genuine. Someone asking what a harness is has not wasted your time.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory comments about people rather than code.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or deliberately wasting maintainers' time.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is representing
this project.

## Enforcement

Report privately through GitHub: on this repository's **Security** tab choose
**Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with `[Conduct]`. Only the maintainers can
see these reports, and they are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close contributions, or ban accounts for
behaviour they judge to breach this document. Where a violation is not clear-cut, the
maintainers will say what they decided and why rather than acting silently.

There is no appeals body — this is a small project. What you get instead is a stated
reason.
